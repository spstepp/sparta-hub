Hymns for a Funeral Program

Most funerals have two or three hymns: one to open, one after the readings or eulogy, and one to close. Amazing Grace, It Is Well with My Soul, How Great Thou Art and Abide with Me are among the most common. In the program, list each hymn by title in the order of service, and print the words only for older hymns or when your church has a license.

Hymns families choose most

  • Amazing Grace (John Newton, 1779)
  • It Is Well with My Soul (Horatio Spafford, 1873)
  • Abide with Me (Henry Francis Lyte, 1847)
  • The Old Rugged Cross (George Bennard, 1913)
  • In the Garden (C. Austin Miles, 1912)
  • How Great Thou Art
  • Be Not Afraid and On Eagle’s Wings, often chosen for Catholic funerals

Where hymns go in the order of service

  • Opening hymn: as the family is seated or after the welcome
  • Middle hymn: after the readings or before the eulogy
  • Closing hymn: before the benediction or as the casket is carried out

Write it as a single line, such as “Hymn: Amazing Grace,” or add the hymnal number if the congregation will sing from a hymnal: “Hymn No. 378, Amazing Grace.”

Printing the words

Hymns from the 1800s and early 1900s, like Amazing Grace and It Is Well with My Soul, are old enough to print freely in most cases. Newer hymns are usually still under copyright, so print only the title unless your church or funeral home has a reprint license. Many churches do; ask the pastor or music director.

Tips

  • Pick the hymn your loved one sang, not just the most popular one
  • Confirm the list with the church musician before you print
  • If the words are printed, put them on an inside page so people can sing along

Quick answers

What is the most popular hymn for a funeral?

Amazing Grace is the most common, followed by hymns like It Is Well with My Soul, How Great Thou Art and Abide with Me.

How many hymns are in a funeral service?

Usually two or three: an opening hymn, one in the middle, and a closing hymn.

Can I print hymn lyrics in a funeral program?

Older hymns such as Amazing Grace are generally free to print. For newer hymns, print the title only unless your church or funeral home has a reprint license.
