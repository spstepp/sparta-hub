Funeral Program vs. Obituary: What’s the Difference?

An obituary announces the death and tells the life story, usually online or in a newspaper before the service. A funeral program is the folded handout guests get at the service, with the order of service and often a shorter version of that same life story.

The obituary

  • Purpose: tells the community that someone has died, and when and where the service will be
  • Where it appears: the funeral home’s website, a local newspaper, or an online memorial page
  • When: usually a few days before the service, so people can plan to come
  • What it includes: full name, age, dates, a short life story, survived by and preceded in death by, service details, and where to send flowers or memorial gifts

The funeral program

  • Purpose: guides guests through the service and gives them something to keep
  • Where it appears: printed and handed out at the door, usually one folded sheet
  • When: printed in the last day or two before the service, once the order is final
  • What it includes: a cover with the photo, name and dates; a life story or obituary; the order of service with names; and a back panel with a poem, pallbearers and a family thank-you

Using the obituary in the program

Most families copy the obituary into the program and trim it to fit one inside panel. Keep the stories and the names of close family. Cut the service times, the newspaper-style survivor lists and the flower instructions if space runs short, since guests are already at the service.

Which one do you need?

Most families do both. The obituary goes out first so people know about the service. The program is for the day itself. If you are short on time, write the obituary first; the program can reuse most of it.

Quick answers

Is a funeral program the same as an obituary?

No. An obituary is a published notice of the death, usually online or in a newspaper. A funeral program is the printed handout at the service. Many programs include a shortened obituary.

Can I put the obituary in the funeral program?

Yes. Most families copy it onto an inside panel and trim it to fit, keeping the life story and close family and cutting details guests no longer need, such as service times.
