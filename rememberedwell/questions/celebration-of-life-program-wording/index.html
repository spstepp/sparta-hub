Celebration of Life Program: What to Say and How to Word It

A celebration of life program is a funeral program with a lighter tone. It still needs the same basics: who, when, and what happens, in order.

What goes on the cover

  • Their full name, dates, and a favorite photo
  • A short line such as “A Celebration of Life” or “Forever in Our Hearts”
  • The date, time and place of the gathering

The inside pages

  • Welcome: one or two sentences thanking people for coming
  • Order of events: music, readings, shared memories, a toast or a moment of silence, then food or a reception
  • Who is speaking: list names and how they knew the person
  • A short obituary or life story: a few lines is enough

The back page

  • A favorite poem, saying or song lyric
  • A thank-you from the family
  • Where to send flowers, or a charity in their name

Tone

Use their own words where you can: a nickname, a favorite joke, a hobby. Plain and warm beats formal.

Quick answers

What is the difference between a funeral and a celebration of life program?

The structure is the same. A celebration of life program usually has a lighter tone, more personal stories and less religious wording.

How long should a celebration of life program be?

One sheet folded in half (four panels) is enough for most gatherings.
