Bible Verses for a Funeral Program

Most families pick one short verse for the back panel or under the photo on the cover. Psalm 23, John 14:1-3, Revelation 21:4 and 2 Timothy 4:7 are the most common. Pick the one your loved one read, or the one that sounds most like them.

Verses families choose most

  • Psalm 23:1 “The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want.” (The full psalm fits a back panel.)
  • John 14:1-2 “Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in God, believe also in me. In my Father’s house are many mansions.”
  • Revelation 21:4 “And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death.”
  • 2 Timothy 4:7 “I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith.”
  • Ecclesiastes 3:1 “To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heaven.”
  • Matthew 5:4 “Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted.”
  • John 11:25 “I am the resurrection, and the life: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live.”

Quotes above are from the King James Version. Many families use the NIV or another translation their church reads; just use one translation throughout.

Matching the verse to the person

  • A long, full life: 2 Timothy 4:7 or Ecclesiastes 3:1
  • A mother or grandmother: Proverbs 31:28, “Her children arise up, and call her blessed”
  • A sudden or hard loss: Psalm 34:18, “The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart”
  • Comfort for the family: John 14:1-2 or Matthew 5:4

Where to put it

  • Under the photo on the cover: one line only
  • Back panel: a longer passage such as Psalm 23, centered, with the reference under it
  • Between the life story and the order of service: a short verse as a break

How to credit it

Put the book, chapter and verse under the text, such as “Psalm 23:1-4.” Add the translation in small type (KJV, NIV) if your church cares about it. Check the wording against a Bible before printing.

Quick answers

What is the most popular Bible verse for a funeral program?

Psalm 23 is the most common, followed by John 14:1-3, Revelation 21:4 and 2 Timothy 4:7.

Where does the Bible verse go in a funeral program?

Usually on the back panel. A one-line verse can also go under the photo on the cover.

Can you use more than one verse in a funeral program?

Yes. Many families put a short verse on the cover and a longer passage on the back. Two is plenty for a folded program.
