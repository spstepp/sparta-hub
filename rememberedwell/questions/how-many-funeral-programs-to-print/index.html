How Many Funeral Programs Should You Print?

There is no fixed rule, but there is a simple way to get close. Start from how many people you expect, then add a few extra.

Start with the guest estimate

  • Ask the funeral home, church or venue how many people the room seats, and how many they think will come
  • Count households, not just people: couples and families often share one program
  • Think about who will come: a large family, a church community, coworkers or a club can each add a lot of guests

Add extras

  • Extra copies for close family to keep, and to mail to people who couldn’t attend
  • A few for the officiant, musicians and readers
  • A small cushion for people who arrive late or ask for a second copy

Running out at the door feels worse than having a stack left over. Leftover programs often end up in scrapbooks and memory boxes.

Printing tips

  • Print one test copy at home, fold it and check names, dates and the cover before printing the rest
  • For a large print run, take the file to a local print or copy shop and ask for double-sided, flip on short edge
  • An editable file lets you reprint later if you need more for a memorial gathering or burial

Quick answers

How many funeral programs do I need?

Start from the number of guests you expect, count by household, then add extras for family keepsakes, the people leading the service and late arrivals.

Can I print funeral programs at home?

Yes. A folded program on US Letter paper prints on a home printer: double-sided, flip on short edge, at 100% or Actual Size. Print one test copy first.
