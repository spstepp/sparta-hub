Funeral Prayer Card Size and What to Print on One

A funeral prayer card is a small keepsake guests take home: a photo or image on the front, and a name, dates and a short prayer or poem on the back.

Size

The most common size is about 2.5 × 4.25 in., close to a playing card, so it fits in a wallet, Bible or purse. Some families choose a larger card, around 3.5 × 5 in., for longer verses. Whatever size you pick, print several cards to a sheet and cut them apart.

Front

  • A photo of your loved one, or a religious image or nature scene
  • Many families keep the front simple: photo only, or photo plus name

Back

  • Full name
  • Birth and death dates
  • A short prayer, verse or poem (keep it to about 8 to 12 lines so the type stays readable)
  • Optional: “In Loving Memory” at the top

Printing

  • Use cardstock, 65 to 110 lb, so the card doesn’t bend
  • Print double-sided at 100% or Actual Size, then print one test sheet to check the backs line up with the fronts
  • Cut with a paper trimmer, not scissors, for straight edges
  • Copy shops can print and cut from a PDF if you are short on time

How many?

Plan on about one card per expected guest plus some extras for family to keep and mail to people who could not attend.

Quick answers

What size is a funeral prayer card?

Most are about 2.5 × 4.25 in., close to a playing card. Some families use a larger card around 3.5 × 5 in. for a longer verse.

What goes on the back of a prayer card?

The person’s full name, birth and death dates, and a short prayer, verse or poem, often under a heading such as “In Loving Memory”.
