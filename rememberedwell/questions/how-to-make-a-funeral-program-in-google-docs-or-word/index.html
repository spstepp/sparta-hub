How to Make a Funeral Program in Google Docs or Word

You don’t need design software. A funeral program is a .docx file you edit like any document, then print on both sides and fold.

Steps

  1. Start from a template. Laying out four panels on a folded sheet by hand is the slow part. A template sets the panels, fonts and photo frame for you.
  2. Open it. In Google Docs, upload the .docx to Google Drive and open it (this works in the free app on a phone, tablet or Chromebook). In Word, open the file; install any free fonts the template lists first.
  3. Type over the sample text. Click any line to change it. To drop a line you don’t need, delete the whole line; to add one, copy an existing line so the style matches.
  4. Add the photo. In Google Docs, click the photo frame, then Replace image. In Word, right-click the frame, then Change Picture.
  5. Proof it. Have someone else read every name, date and spelling.
  6. Print. US Letter (8.5 × 11 in.), double-sided, “flip on short edge”, at 100% or Actual Size, not “Fit to page”. Bright white 32 lb paper or 65–80 lb cardstock looks good. Print one test copy and fold it before printing them all.
  7. Fold. Fold each sheet in half to 5.5 × 8.5 in. Most office-supply and copy shops can print and fold from a PDF if you’re short on time.

Quick answers

Can I make a funeral program on my phone?

Yes. A .docx template opens in the free Google Docs app on a phone or tablet, where you can type over the text and replace the photo.

How do I print a funeral program double-sided?

Use US Letter paper, choose two-sided printing with “flip on short edge”, and print at 100% or Actual Size. Print one test copy and fold it to check the cover is on the front.
