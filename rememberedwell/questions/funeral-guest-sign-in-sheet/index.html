What Is the Book Called That You Sign at a Funeral?

The book guests sign at a funeral is usually called a guest book or a memorial register. Many families now print a guest sign-in sheet instead, and set it on a table with a pen.

What it’s called

People also say funeral guest book, memorial register or sign-in book. A printable version is a guest sign-in sheet or funeral sign-in sheet. It does the same job as a bound book: a record of who came, and often a short message.

What to put on the page

  • A heading such as “In Loving Memory,” plus the person’s name and dates
  • A short line inviting people to sign and share a memory
  • Columns for Name, Address or Relationship, and Message

How many pages

Print a first page with the heading, then as many continuation pages as you need. Keep extras at the table so the line doesn’t stop if a sheet fills up. After the service, the pages can go in a folder or binder.

How to make one

  • Edit a .docx file in free Google Docs (even on a phone or Chromebook) or in Microsoft Word
  • Type the name and dates over the sample text
  • Print on US Letter paper at 100% or Actual Size. The sheet does not need to be folded

Guest book or sign-in sheet?

A bound guest book is a physical book you buy. A sign-in sheet is a printable page you can run off at home or at a copy shop, then keep with the program and thank-you list. The sign-in sheet also makes it easier later to see who should get a thank-you card.

Quick answers

What is the book called that you sign at a funeral?

It is usually called a guest book or a memorial register. A printable alternative is a funeral guest sign-in sheet.

What do you put on a funeral sign-in sheet?

The person’s name and dates at the top, a short invitation to sign, and columns for Name, Address or Relationship, and a Message.
