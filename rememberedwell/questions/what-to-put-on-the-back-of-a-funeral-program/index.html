What to Put on the Back of a Funeral Program

The back of a funeral program usually holds four things: a short poem or verse, the names of the pallbearers, a thank-you from the family, and where guests should go after the service.

What goes on the back panel

  • A poem or verse: 4 to 12 lines fits well. Psalm 23, “Do Not Stand at My Grave and Weep” and “Miss Me, But Let Me Go” are common picks.
  • Pallbearers: list each name, then honorary pallbearers under their own heading if you have them.
  • Family thank-you: two or three sentences thanking people for coming, for the food, the cards and the prayers.
  • After the service: the cemetery name and address for the burial, and where the reception or meal is, with a time.
  • In lieu of flowers (optional): the charity or fund the family has picked.
  • Funeral home (optional): one small line, such as “Arrangements by Riverside Funeral Home.”

A sample family thank-you

The family of Margaret Ellis thanks you for your calls, cards, food and prayers, and for being here today. Your kindness has carried us through.
We are grateful to everyone who loved Dad and showed up for him, and for us. Please join us after the burial for lunch at the church hall.

If it doesn't all fit

Keep the thank-you and the after-service details, since guests need those that day. A short verse can move to the cover or the inside left panel. Honorary pallbearers can be cut first if space is tight.

What not to put there

Leave off the order of service (that goes inside) and long lists of survivors (that belongs in the obituary). Skip the full address of the family home.

Quick answers

What do you put on the back of a funeral program?

Most families put a short poem or verse, the pallbearers, a thank-you from the family, and details for the burial and reception.

Where do the pallbearers go in a funeral program?

Usually on the back panel, under a heading such as Pallbearers, with honorary pallbearers listed separately below them.

What do you write for the acknowledgement in a funeral program?

Two or three plain sentences: thank people for coming, for their cards, food and prayers, and for their support. Sign it from the family of the person who died.
