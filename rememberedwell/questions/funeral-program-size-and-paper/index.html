What Size Is a Funeral Program? Paper and Fold Guide

The most common funeral program is one sheet of US Letter paper (8.5 x 11 inches) folded in half, which makes a 5.5 x 8.5 inch booklet with four panels.

Common sizes

  • Half-fold on US Letter: 8.5 x 11 in, folded to 5.5 x 8.5 in. Four panels: cover, two inside panels, back. Prints on any home printer.
  • Half-fold on legal: 8.5 x 14 in, folded to 7 x 8.5 in. A little more room inside. Check that your printer takes legal paper.
  • Tabloid: 11 x 17 in, folded to 8.5 x 11 in. A larger booklet, usually printed at a print shop.
  • Single card or flat sheet: for a short service or a simple handout.

Paper

  • Cardstock or heavier paper feels more like a keepsake and folds crisply
  • Matte paper is easier to read under church or chapel lights than glossy
  • Check your printer’s maximum paper weight before buying cardstock

Print settings

  • Double-sided, flip on short edge
  • Scale at 100% or Actual Size, not “fit to page”
  • Print and fold one test copy to make sure the cover lands on the front

Quick answers

What size is a standard funeral program?

Most are US Letter (8.5 x 11 inches) folded in half to 5.5 x 8.5 inches, giving four panels.

What paper is best for funeral programs?

Matte cardstock or a heavier paper your printer can handle. It folds cleanly and holds up as a keepsake.
