Sparta Labs is a small studio with five independent projects.

Projects

  • Spreadsheet templates

    Spreadsheet templates for therapists and small practices, made for Excel and Google Sheets. They help you keep track of clients, insurance visits, billing, and license renewal.

    See the templates

  • Babette's Table

    English translations of a 1956 Bavarian cookbook, sold as PDF volumes. Each volume has the recipes, a bit of their history, and measurements you can use in a US kitchen.

    Visit the site

  • CashRate Watch

    A weekly list of places to keep cash in the US, including high-yield savings, money market funds, Treasury bills, broker cash, and USDC rewards. Each one has a plain risk label, and the rates come from the providers' own pages.

    Visit the site

  • Houseform Studios

    Simple, vintage-style posters of mountain towns like Telluride, Creede, and McCall, plus a personalized Goldendoodle print. They make good wall art or gifts, and you order them on Etsy.

    Visit the site

  • RememberedWellCo

    Editable funeral programs and celebration-of-life templates, with matching prayer cards, thank-you cards and sign-in sheets. You can edit them in Google Docs or Word, print them at home, and buy them on Etsy.
